NEXUS is a map-first social platform for adults. These standards describe conduct we prohibit, how concerns may be reported, and the child-safety actions NEXUS must be able to take.

1. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

NEXUS strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Prohibited activity includes grooming, sextortion, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking, abuse, and using the service to facilitate or coordinate these acts.

Users must not create, upload, request, store, share, link to, distribute, promote, or facilitate such content or activity through profiles, posts, images, messages, stories, events, listings, audio, video, or external links. This prohibition also applies to AI-generated or manipulated material where it is exploitative or illegal.

2. Reporting child-safety concerns

If a user encounters suspected exploitation, they should report the relevant account or content using the in-app reporting controls where available. The operator also accepts safety concerns by email at vganchev6@gmail.com. The email address is NEXUS's existing publicly documented contact; the developer-account child-safety contact in Google Play Console must be independently confirmed.

Include only the minimum information needed to identify a concern (for example, a username, content reference, and a brief description). Do not download, attach, copy, or forward suspected CSAM. Reporting tools are not emergency services. If a child faces immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

3. Safety response and enforcement

Reports suggesting child exploitation require priority review by an authorized operator. When NEXUS obtains actual knowledge of CSAM, appropriate action must include restricting access to or removing the material in accordance with applicable law, evaluating account restrictions, limiting further distribution where technically possible, and escalating confirmed cases to competent authorities as required by law.

Any evidence preservation, disclosure, retention, and removal must follow applicable legal requirements and minimize unnecessary handling of sensitive material. NEXUS does not promise continuous 24/7 monitoring or an instant response. The internal incident-handling process and operational ability to carry out these actions require validation before these standards become effective.

4. Adults-only release and location safety

The intended first release of NEXUS Social is for users aged 18 and older. Dating and matchmaking are outside the first-release scope. An asserted date of birth by itself does not independently verify a person's age; launch restrictions must be enforced and verified across sign-in, APIs, and real-time connections.

Public discovery uses privacy-coarsened map presence. Optional live precise location sharing in one-to-one chat can reveal and store precise coordinates; it is distinct from public discovery. Users should not share a live location with someone they do not trust. See the NEXUS Privacy Policy for details.

5. User protections and changes

NEXUS prohibits attempts to evade safety restrictions, harass reporters, or retaliate against people who raise good-faith concerns. Account blocking, reporting, visibility, and account deletion controls must be verified on the release build. Safety measures may be updated as the service, legal requirements, and moderation capabilities evolve.
