NEXUS Child Safety Standards

Version 1.0 · Published 26 November 2026

NEXUS is intended to be an adult-only social service. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation and take reports of child-safety concerns seriously. These standards describe our rules and reporting expectations; they do not promise continuous monitoring or a particular response time.

1. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

NEXUS prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, trafficking or exploitation of children, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and attempts to arrange, promote or facilitate such conduct. This prohibition applies to profiles, text, images, audio, video, Stories, messages, events, listings, external links and interactions. Altered or AI-generated material is not exempt where it depicts or facilitates illegal or exploitative conduct involving a child.

Users must not create, request, upload, store, share, distribute, promote, link to or use NEXUS to facilitate prohibited content or conduct. Do not retaliate against good-faith safety reporters.

2. Adult-only eligibility

The intended first-release NEXUS Social service is restricted to people aged 18 or older. Under-18 users may not create or use accounts. Dating and matchmaking are not part of the initial release. A self-declared date of birth is an age declaration, not independent identity or age verification. The effectiveness of age restrictions across every access path is subject to ongoing testing and enforcement.

3. Reporting

Report a concerning account or supported user-generated content using the in-app reporting controls where available. You may also contact the designated child-safety contact at vganchev6@gmail.com. Provide only the minimum details needed to identify the account or content and describe the concern.

Do not download, attach, copy, forward or redistribute suspected CSAM or other illegal imagery. Do not send illegal imagery by email. If a child appears to be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement directly. NEXUS reporting is not an emergency service and is not a guarantee of immediate response.

4. Review and moderation

Child-safety reports may be reviewed and acted on according to severity, available controls, evidence and applicable law. Where appropriate, actions may include restricting content or accounts, preventing abusive contact and escalation to competent authorities. We may take further action against repeat violations or attempts to evade enforcement.

We do not claim 24/7 monitoring, a fixed response time, automatic CSAM detection, automatic account suspension or immediate removal from all caches or devices. Reports and decisions should be handled with restricted access and appropriate data minimization. Where legally required and appropriate, we may report suspected exploitation to competent authorities. We do not ask users to send or retain suspected CSAM as evidence.

5. Safety, privacy and location

Public nearby discovery is designed to use coarsened location. This does not guarantee anonymity. Opt-in precise live-location sharing in a one-to-one chat can disclose precise coordinates to the selected recipient and may result in stored location records. Share precise location only with someone you trust. See the Privacy Policy.

6. Blocking, account controls and updates

Use in-app report and block controls where available. Account visibility and deletion controls may also be available. Safety reports should not require acceptance of updated content-posting terms. We may update these standards as our service, procedures or legal obligations change.
