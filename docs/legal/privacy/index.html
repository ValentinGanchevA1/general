NEXUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Operated by Valentin Ganchev (“NEXUS”, “we”, “us”).

NEXUS is a map-first, location-based social app: nearby people and local activity appear on a live map so you can discover, connect, chat, join events, and explore local listings. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. Privacy is a core design constraint of the product: most notably, your public map presence is privacy-coarsened. Separately, if you explicitly activate live location sharing in a one-to-one chat, your chat partner can see precise GPS coordinates, which are also stored by our service as explained below.

1. Who this applies to

This policy explains how the NEXUS mobile application and backend services process data. General NEXUS social features are not restricted to adults only. Dating and matchmaking are reserved for users aged at least 18 years old. Account registration does not currently request a date of birth. A birth date is optional for ordinary profile features. Dating opt-in requires a declared date of birth to confirm 18+ eligibility. The backend checks adult eligibility for Dating opt-in, Dating interactions and Dating-only discovery. A self-reported birth date is not independent age or identity verification. Additional safeguards may be needed to prevent false age declarations, and age-appropriate protections for younger users require separate review.

3. Location — how we protect it

Your device determines your precise GPS position for nearby discovery. That precise position is transmitted to our server over an encrypted connection and is immediately coarsened to roughly a 120-meter area (an H3 grid-cell centroid) before public map-presence data is stored. In nearby map discovery, other users see only the privacy-coarsened position rather than your raw GPS coordinates. Opt-in direct-message live-location sharing is different; that feature stores and discloses precise location as described below.

Opt-in chat live location: In a one-to-one chat, you can explicitly choose to share your precise GPS position with that chat partner for 15 minutes, 60 minutes, or until you stop. Your partner can see accurate coordinates while the sharing session remains active. The service stores the latest precise location in the chat-location session record and the initial shared coordinates in the chat message. Ending the session stops new updates but does not automatically remove these stored records. Records are handled under account/chat retention and deletion procedures below; please do not share location unless you trust the recipient. Group chats cannot use this feature.

Location is used only while you are using the app (foreground); NEXUS does not track your location in the background. You can turn your map presence off from You → My map presence, and you can revoke the OS location permission in your device settings (nearby discovery that depends on location will stop working while it is off).

4. How we use your data

  • Operate core features: discovery, presence, waves, chat, events, listings, gifts, challenges, stories and voluntary dating interactions.
  • Receive and moderate reports of user-generated content to prevent spam, abuse and harassment.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Assess adult eligibility for optional Dating and matchmaking only; general social access does not require a universally adult profile. A declared birth date is not proof of age, and further safeguards may be necessary.
  • Send notifications you have not opted out of (you control these per-channel in Settings).
  • Maintain reliability and diagnose crashes and errors.
  • Understand aggregate product usage to improve NEXUS.
  • Process verification and subscriptions when you choose to use them.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not show third-party advertising.

5. Who we share with

Other users see your display name, avatar/photos, bio, interests, verification signals, and your coarsened map presence when enabled. They do not receive your email, phone or date of birth. An individual chat recipient can receive your exact location only when you explicitly start a live-location share with them. Your age is shown only when your profile visibility setting for age is enabled. Your optional sexual orientation is only shown if its independent visibility setting is enabled; dating seeking preferences are not publicly exposed as profile fields.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Diagnostic and analytics data are retained for a limited period for security and product purposes. When you delete your account, your data is removed as described below. Presence data is ephemeral and expires automatically within minutes.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. Account deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Associated storage cleanup may continue asynchronously if a storage provider is temporarily unavailable.
  • By web request: visit NEXUS account deletion requests to initiate a request outside the app.
  • By email request: email vganchev6@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Delete my account” and we will complete the deletion.

Deletion removes your profile, photos and uploaded documents, messages, waves, listings, events, gifts, gamification progress, verification records, and authentication sessions from active application systems. Some storage objects may remain briefly while retry cleanup completes, some records may persist briefly in encrypted backups before being overwritten, and we may retain the minimum needed to comply with legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise most of these in the app, or by contacting us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS, secure WebSockets). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; authentication tokens on your device are held in the OS secure keystore. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to minimise what we collect (for example, by coarsening location before storage).

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in regions including the EU and the US. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
