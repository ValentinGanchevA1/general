NEXA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Operated by Valentin Ganchev (“NEXA”, “we”, “us”).

NEXA is a map-first, location-based social app: nearby people and local activity appear on a live map so you can discover, connect, chat, join events, and explore local listings. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. Privacy is a core design constraint of the product: most notably, your exact location is never shared with other users or stored on our servers (see “Location” below).

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the NEXA mobile application and its backend services. By creating an account or using NEXA you agree to it. NEXA is for adults; you must be at least 18 years old to use it. The onboarding flow requires a date of birth and the service rejects profiles that do not meet this minimum age.

3. Location — how we protect it

Your device determines your precise GPS position for nearby discovery. That precise position is transmitted to our server over an encrypted connection and is immediately coarsened to roughly a 120-meter area (an H3 grid-cell centroid) before anything is stored. We do not retain your exact GPS coordinates, and other users only ever see the coarsened location. This means you appear “in the neighborhood,” never at a precise point.

Location is used only while you are using the app (foreground); NEXA does not track your location in the background. You can turn your map presence off from You → My map presence, and you can revoke the OS location permission in your device settings (nearby discovery that depends on location will stop working while it is off).

4. How we use your data

  • Operate core features: discovery, presence, waves, chat, events, listings, gifts, challenges.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Confirm that the profile meets NEXA’s 18+ eligibility requirement.
  • Send notifications you have not opted out of (you control these per-channel in Settings).
  • Maintain reliability and diagnose crashes and errors.
  • Understand aggregate product usage to improve NEXA.
  • Process verification and subscriptions when you choose to use them.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not show third-party advertising.

5. Who we share with

Other users see your display name, avatar/photos, bio, interests, verification signals, and your coarsened (~120 m) location when map presence is enabled — not your email, phone, date of birth, or precise location. Your age is shown only when your profile visibility setting for age is enabled.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Diagnostic and analytics data are retained for a limited period for security and product purposes. When you delete your account, your data is removed as described below. Presence data is ephemeral and expires automatically within minutes.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. Account deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Associated storage cleanup may continue asynchronously if a storage provider is temporarily unavailable.
  • By web/email request: email vganchev6@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Delete my account” and we will complete the deletion.

Deletion removes your profile, photos and uploaded documents, messages, waves, listings, events, gifts, gamification progress, verification records, and authentication sessions from active application systems. Some storage objects may remain briefly while retry cleanup completes, some records may persist briefly in encrypted backups before being overwritten, and we may retain the minimum needed to comply with legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise most of these in the app, or by contacting us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS, secure WebSockets). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; authentication tokens on your device are held in the OS secure keystore. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to minimise what we collect (for example, by coarsening location before storage).

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in regions including the EU and the US. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
