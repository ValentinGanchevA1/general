Request deletion of your NEXA account and associated data

You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to it. This page provides an external request method for NEXA account holders.

Option 1 — In the NEXA app

Open Settings → Delete account and follow the confirmation flow.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send a request to the NEXA support contact. When possible, write from the email address associated with your NEXA account. If you no longer have access to that email, explain this and we will help verify ownership before processing the request.

Email NEXA to request deletion

Email: vganchev6@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, copies of identity documents, or payment-card details.

What is deleted?

We process account and associated-data deletion according to the NEXA Privacy Policy, including its retention and backup exceptions. We may need to verify account ownership to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Questions? Contact NEXA support.
